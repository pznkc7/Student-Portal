About This Portal

The Student Leave Management System was developed to streamline and digitize the leave application process at Everest Institute of Technology. Students can submit leave requests online, track their status, and receive updates — all without paperwork. Faculty and administrators can review, approve, and manage applications from a central dashboard, improving transparency and efficiency across all departments.
